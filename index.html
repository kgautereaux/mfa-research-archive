    Research supervision

    The research journey for our MFA students is a three-semester sequence from proposal to data collection and analysis to revisions and defense. Independent studies are completed by MM students interested in participating in research. Methods span acoustic analysis, physiological monitoring, perceptual evaluation, survey and interview, and mixed methods.
